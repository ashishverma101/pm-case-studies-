Excited to share my first Product Management Case Study!

As a small creator myself, I experienced something many creators face:

📉 Consistent uploads but low impressions
📉 Quality content but unpredictable reach
📉 Great ideas struggling to find the right audience

That made me ask an important product question:

How can YouTube improve discovery for small creators?

Over the past few days, I explored this challenge from a Product Manager's perspective and created a case study covering:

🔍 Problem definition and market context
👥 User research and creator personas
💡 Product solutions for improving discoverability
🖼️ Wireframes and feature concepts
📊 Success metrics and business impact

Some ideas I proposed:

• Hyper-Local Discovery – helping viewers discover creators from their city or region.
• Collab Matchmaker – connecting small creators with complementary creators for growth opportunities.
• Early Performance Feedback – actionable insights immediately after publishing.
• Smarter Creator Analytics – moving beyond numbers to explain why videos underperform and how to improve them.

Being both a creator and an aspiring Product Manager made this case study especially meaningful for me because it was built around a problem I have personally faced with my channel, Ashish Snaps.
